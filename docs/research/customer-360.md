# What a customer overview looks like elsewhere

Read 17 September 2026, to answer one question for
[the conversation view](../plans/conversation-view.md): when you open a
customer, what should be on the screen, and where do activities sit.

Five products, what each one does, and the one thing worth taking from it.

## HubSpot: three columns, and tabs in the middle

[The default record layout](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/understand-the-default-record-layout)
is the most copied shape in the category.

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Left | Highlights (name, email) and the properties you edit |
| Middle | Tabs: About, **Activities**, Catch-up, Revenue, Intelligence |
| Right | Associations (company, deals, tickets) and attachments |

The Activities tab is "a timeline of activities and engagements on the record",
with a filter row above it (Notes, Emails, Calls) and expand or collapse all.

**Take:** the tab row. One record, several readings of it, one at a time. It is
also how they keep the timeline from becoming a dump.

**Leave:** Catch-up and Intelligence. Catch-up is "insights, health, and data
quality... generated by Breeze", which is a score and a summary over somebody's
correspondence. We removed our AI seam in 19.0.7.0.0 and this is not the reason
to bring it back.

## Salesforce: the timeline has a future half

The [activity timeline](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?language=en_US&id=sales.activity_timeline_parent.htm&type=5)
puts **upcoming** activities above **past** ones on the same axis: open tasks
and future meetings on top, completed calls, emails and events below.

**Take:** the split. A pure history answers "what happened" and never answers
"what now", which is the question somebody opening a customer actually has.
Odoo already has the object for the top half: `mail.activity`, with a deadline
and a person.

**Leave:** the configuration surface. Salesforce lets an admin tailor the
timeline per object, which is a setting nobody in a five-person company will
ever open.

## Attio: the timeline is one place, and it is filtered

Their [new activity timeline](https://attio.com/changelog/2026/new-activity-timeline)
merged tasks into the record history so "the work associated with a record"
lives with its history. Stated reason: **"Eliminate noise in your records"**,
with a per-object filter that remembers what you chose.

**Take:** merge, then filter. One axis for mail, activities and the record's
own events, with a filter row rather than three separate lists. And the
sentence: a 360 view fails by showing everything, not by showing too little.

## Front: the context panel beside the conversation

Front shows a contact's past threads and details in a right-hand panel while
you read a message, and lets plugins put CRM fields there.

**Take:** this is the pane we already designed, from the other side. Front
brings a thin copy of the CRM to the mail; we have the real record, so our
fourth pane wins on content and must not lose on speed.

## Odoo itself: a row of counters

`res.partner` already has the smart-button row (Meetings, Sales, Invoiced,
Opportunities, Tickets, Tasks), the chatter, and the activity clock. That is
Odoo's customer 360 and it has two problems: every button is a count that
sends you to a different list, and none of them is the correspondence. Nobody
reads a company by clicking six buttons.

**Take:** the counts are right, the destination is wrong. The numbers belong on
one screen that opens in place.

## What this means for our customer view

**The decision: two tabs, not five.**

1. **Conversations.** Threads with this company, across records and mailboxes.
   The default, because mail is why the screen exists.
2. **Timeline.** Everything on one axis, newest first: messages, activities
   done, and the record events that a person would mention out loud (quote
   sent, order confirmed, invoice paid). With **Next** pinned above it: the
   open activities with a deadline and an owner, which is Salesforce's future
   half and Odoo's `mail.activity` unchanged.

A filter row over the timeline, with three chips (Mail, Activities, Record
events) and nothing remembered per object. Attio saves the preference; we have
three chips, so the state is cheap to re-set and expensive to explain.

**What we refuse, and why each one is somebody's favourite feature:**

- **A health score or an AI summary of the relationship.** It is a fact nobody
  can check, over correspondence, generated for a screen that is supposed to be
  a visualisation layer.
- **Enrichment.** Company data bought from a third party, which is a different
  product with a different privacy posture.
- **A "last contacted" field on the partner.** It looks harmless and it is a
  stored fact that goes stale the moment a sync is late. Derived from the
  messages, same as waiting-on-us.
- **Per-object timeline configuration.** One layout, chosen by us.

Everything above reads rows that already exist: `mail.message` for the mail,
`mail.activity` for the next steps, and the record's own tracked-field messages
for the events. Nothing new is stored, which is the rule the whole view is
built on.
